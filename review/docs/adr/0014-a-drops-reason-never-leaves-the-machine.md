# 14. A drop's reason never leaves the machine

- Status: accepted
- Date: 2026-09-09
- Part of: reviewing a branch, and reviewing a change that is not mine

## Decision

The reason a finding was dropped is kept on the run record and is never posted.
The author of the change learns which findings were raised and nothing about which were considered and dismissed.
Every finding still gets a card of its own on the page, so a person still signs off on the round rather than on what survived triage.
