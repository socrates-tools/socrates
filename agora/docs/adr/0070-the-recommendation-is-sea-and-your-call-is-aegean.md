# 70. The recommendation is sea and your call is Aegean

- Status: accepted, its Your call mark drawn outlined by ADR 89
- Date: 2026-10-15
- Amends: ADR 53
- Part of: the-aegean-page

## Decision

The mark that says an option is recommended is drawn in sea, a teal ink on a teal wash.
The mark that says a decision is *Your call* is drawn in Aegean, the accent's ink on the accent's wash, in a tone named for what it says rather than for its hue.

Clay marks only the calls in code.
Everything else ADR 53 decided still holds.

## Rejected

- Keeping both marks in clay.
  On one card the two read as one signal, so a person could not tell at a glance that the decision is theirs from which option the caller would take.
- Drawing *Your call* in clay and only the recommendation in sea.
  Terracotta beside the cinnabar of an error reads as a second warning, and *Your call* asks a person to decide rather than warns them.
