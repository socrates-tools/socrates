# 2.2: A silent stream is dropped

## What to build

The surface pings each stream about every thirty seconds and closes one whose pong does not arrive in time, so a page that vanished without closing its socket, such as one behind a forward on a machine that went to sleep, stops counting as a reader and the session can be collected.
The interval and the wait are stated once beside the surface's other bounds on waiting, and agora's instructions gain an invariant for the ping.

## Acceptance criteria

- [ ] A stream that stops answering pings is closed by the surface.
- [ ] Once that stream is closed, the session it was reading is collected after its idle period like any session with no reader.
- [ ] A stream whose page answers pings stays open past several intervals.
