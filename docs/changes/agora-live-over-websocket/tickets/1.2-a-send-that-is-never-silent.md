# 1.2: A send that is never silent

## What to build

A send is visible however it ends.
Both presses are disabled while a send is in flight, so a second press queues nothing.
A send the surface has not answered within one minute is aborted, and a request the browser rejects is caught, and either way the page says plainly that nothing was sent and why it might be, in the tone it already uses for a refused answer.
The presses are enabled again and nothing the person chose, wrote or attached is cleared.
The new sentences live with the page's other messages, and agora's instructions gain an invariant that a send is never silent.

## Acceptance criteria

- [ ] While a send is in flight, both presses are disabled, and pressing again sends no second request.
- [ ] A send the surface holds unanswered for a minute is aborted, and the page says nothing was sent and why it might be.
- [ ] A send the browser rejects says nothing was sent, rather than staying on "Sending.".
- [ ] After a send that timed out or was rejected, both presses are enabled again and every choice, word and attachment is still on the page.
