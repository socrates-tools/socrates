# 2.1: The page says it lost the surface

## What to build

When a page's stream closes or cannot open, the page says it lost the surface and is trying again, and reconnects with a backoff that starts near a second and doubles to a cap near thirty seconds.
When it reconnects, the status returns to where the exchange stands and any revision it missed swaps in.
When the surface answers that it holds no such session, the page closes the exchange, says the session is gone and stops trying.
The new sentences live with the page's other messages, and agora's instructions gain an invariant for a page that loses the surface.

## Acceptance criteria

- [ ] After `socrates agora stop`, an open page says it lost the surface and is trying again.
- [ ] A page whose surface comes back with its session still held reconnects by itself, its status returns to where the exchange stands, and a revision served while it was gone swaps in.
- [ ] A page whose session the surface no longer holds says the session is gone, offers nothing to answer, and stops reconnecting.
