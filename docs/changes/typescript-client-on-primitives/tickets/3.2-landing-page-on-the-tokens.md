# 3.2: The landing page is painted by the library's tokens

## What to build

`make site` copies the library's tokens, its font faces and its fonts into the site, and the landing page loads the tokens ahead of its own stylesheet.
The site's stylesheet drops its own copy of the palette and its own font faces, and every name only the site used moves to the library's name for the same colour.
Its layout stays its own.
A colour or a font family written in the site's stylesheet fails, as it does for the client.

## Acceptance criteria

- [ ] The site assembled by `make site` loads the library's `tokens.css`, and its stylesheet declares no palette and no font face of its own.
- [ ] Every colour the landing page paints is the library's, and the page looks as it did, checked by a person comparing it before and after.
- [ ] A colour literal or a font family written in the site's stylesheet fails the suite, naming the file.
- [ ] Changing a colour in the library's tokens changes it on both the page and the landing page.
