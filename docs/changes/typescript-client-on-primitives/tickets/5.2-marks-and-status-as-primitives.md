# 5.2: Badges, progress, status and tooltips are primitives

## What to build

The badge, the progress bar, the status dot, the tooltip, the overline and the card move into the library as primitives, each with its partial, its contract entry and its states in the catalog.
The mark becomes a piece of the surface drawn on the badge, the index and the topbar are rebuilt on the progress bar, the status dot and the overline, and the decision is rebuilt on the card.
The tooltip's behaviour on hover, on focus and on scroll belongs to the tooltip primitive.

## Acceptance criteria

- [ ] Each of the six is declared in the contract, drawn by one partial, and drawn in every state in the catalog.
- [ ] The browser suite proves the tooltip against the catalog, showing on hover and on focus and hiding on scroll.
- [ ] Every mark still says what it means on hover, on focus and to a screen reader, and the browser tests about marks, the index and the status pass unchanged.
- [ ] The page's stylesheet no longer styles any of the six itself.
