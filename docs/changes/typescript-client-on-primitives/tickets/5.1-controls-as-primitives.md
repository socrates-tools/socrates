# 5.1: The page's controls are primitives

## What to build

The button, the text field, the choice an option is drawn as, the copy control and the expand control move into the library as primitives, each a custom element with its partial, its contract entry and its states in the catalog.
The pieces agora's glossary names that use them, the option, the composer, the evidence and the illustration's frame, are rebuilt on them and keep their own classes.

## Acceptance criteria

- [ ] Each of the five is declared in the contract, drawn by one partial, and drawn in every state in the catalog.
- [ ] The browser suite proves each one against the catalog: pressing, typing, choosing, copying and expanding.
- [ ] The page's send, finish, options, words field, copy and expand behave as before, and the browser tests about them pass unchanged.
- [ ] The page's stylesheet no longer styles any of the five itself.
