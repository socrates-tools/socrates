# 5.4: The app shell is a primitive

## What to build

The app shell moves into the library as a primitive: the topbar, the navigation that scrolls on its own, the decisions that scroll on their own, and the reply panel's place from 1280px.
The surface is drawn inside it, and the navigation's marking of the decision in view belongs to the shell.

## Acceptance criteria

- [ ] The shell is declared in the contract, drawn by one partial, and drawn in the catalog at a narrow width, at 960px and at 1280px.
- [ ] The browser suite proves against the catalog that from 960px only the navigation and the content scroll, and that the navigation marks the section in view.
- [ ] The browser tests about the shell, the index and the reply panel pass unchanged.
- [ ] The page's stylesheet no longer lays out the shell itself.
