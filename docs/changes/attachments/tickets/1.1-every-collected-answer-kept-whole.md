# 1.1: Every collected answer is kept whole in its session

## What to build

Collecting an answer keeps it in its own numbered folder of the session's directory, so every answer a session collected stays on disk until the surface collects the session, per ADR 88.
`socrates agora poll --last` keeps printing the last answer collected, without waiting and without the surface.
`agora/AGENTS.md` states the invariant in place of the one-answer.json rule of ADR 73.

## Acceptance criteria

- [ ] After two answers are collected in one session, both are on disk under the session's directory, each in a folder of its own numbered in the order collected.
- [ ] `socrates agora poll --last` prints the second answer, not the first, with no surface running.
- [ ] An answer the session could not keep stays uncollected, and the next poll collects it.
- [ ] The session's directory, every collected answer included, is removed when the surface collects the session.
