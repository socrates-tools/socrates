# 2.2: The last read reviews the whole change

## What to build

Once the last ticket has landed and passed, the overseer puts socrates review in front of the whole change and nobody is asked anything.
She begins the review on the change's branch, in the person's own checkout, against the default branch, with `--nobody-to-ask`, an intent that says the change builds its spec, and her own host, and walks the spec's user stories while the reviewer reads.
She settles each finding that asks a person by her own judgement, rejects each she judges false, and hands every fix and every story the change falls short of to one fresh implementer together, landed and verified the way a ticket is.
She then records a response to every finding and runs the second round, unless the first ended clean.
Where the review ends escalated or failed, she starts no third run and hands back anyway.
The hand-back says how the review ended, lists every finding still open or the failure the reviewer gave, and lists each settled finding with its reason, beside what it already lists.
She runs review's commands and never a skill, and the implementer's definition and Codex role stay free of every socrates command.
This holds in both hosts' renderings, and implement's invariants and ADR 49's guard change to match ADR 90.

## Acceptance criteria

- [ ] The installed socrates-implement skill, for each host, tells the overseer to begin `socrates review` over the whole change at the last read with `--base` naming the default branch, `--nobody-to-ask`, `--intent` pointing at `docs/changes/<slug>/spec.md` and `--host`; today it names no socrates review command.
- [ ] It tells her to send every review fix and every story shortfall to one fresh implementer together, and to land and verify that work as a ticket's before the second round.
- [ ] It tells her to answer a finding that asks a person with `--settled`, never `--decided`, and to record a response to every finding before `socrates review continue`.
- [ ] It tells her that an escalated or failed review starts no third run and is still handed back, with the verdict and what is still open or the failure given.
- [ ] Its hand-back lists each settled finding with its reason.
- [ ] Every `socrates review` command and flag the skill names exists in the binary, and a skill naming one the binary lacks fails the suite.
- [ ] The skill still names no skill to invoke: a rendering naming `/socrates-review` or `$socrates-review` fails the suite.
- [ ] The implementer's agent definition and Codex role still name no `socrates` command.
