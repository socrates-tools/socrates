# 2.3: A decision's choices as one ui-choice-group

## What to build

A decision's options and its *None of these* are `<ui-choice>` Lit elements slotted into one new `<ui-choice-group>`, each choice rendering into a shadow root.
The group keeps at most one choice checked in a single-choice decision, moves the checked choice and the focus on the arrow keys, keeps one tab stop, and is announced as a radio group with each choice's state, and a multiple-choice decision is a group of checkboxes.
The option a document marks still arrives chosen, and what is sent and what the index counts are as they are today.

## Acceptance criteria

- [ ] Checking one choice of a single-choice decision unchecks the one checked before, including *None of these*.
- [ ] The arrow keys move the checked choice and the focus within a single-choice decision, and Tab enters the group at its checked choice and leaves it in one press.
- [ ] A multiple-choice decision checks several choices at once.
- [ ] A screen reader is told a single-choice decision is a radio group and each choice whether it is checked, and a multiple-choice decision's choices are checkboxes.
- [ ] The recommended option arrives chosen, *None of these* clears the selection and moves the cursor into the words field, and a closed exchange draws every choice disabled.
- [ ] The catalog draws the group in every state it has, and every choice state looks as it did before.
- [ ] The group's and the choice's partials write only their elements, declared attributes and slotted content, held by the Go contract test.
