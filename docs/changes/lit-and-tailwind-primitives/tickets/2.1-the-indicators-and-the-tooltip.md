# 2.1: The indicators, the brand and the tooltip as Lit elements

## What to build

The status dot, the overline, the progress bar, the brand and the tooltip are Lit elements rendering into shadow roots, styled with utilities, and the index, the topbar and every tooltip on the page look and behave as they do today.
The tooltip still shows on hover and on focus of anything carrying `data-title`, including a primitive's own element, hides when the cursor or the focus leaves and on scroll, and follows a focused anchor when the page scrolls.

## Acceptance criteria

- [ ] Each of the status dot, the overline, the progress bar, the brand and the tooltip has a shadow root on the page and in the catalog, and every state the catalog draws of each looks as it did before.
- [ ] The index counts and marks decisions as answered exactly as it did, as a person answers them.
- [ ] A tooltip shows for a `data-title` on a primitive's element as it does for one on a plain element, on hover and on focus, and hides on leaving and on scroll.
- [ ] Each one's partial writes only the element, its declared attributes and its slotted content, held by the Go contract test.
